Questions

Does it actually block apps?+

Yes. When you open a blocked app, Broc's wall appears over it and stays until your habits are done. There is no snooze and no "5 more minutes."

What does it cost?+

Broc is a subscription with a 7-day free trial. One plan includes unlimited habits, custom unlock durations, photo verification, and progress history. No ads, ever.

What does Broc see on my phone?+

On Android, Broc checks which app is open against your block list. On iPhone, Apple keeps your Screen Time selections opaque. No keylogging, no screen recording, no browsing history. Details in the privacy policy.

Can't I just uninstall it?+

You can. Broc isn't a parental-control cage, it's a wall you chose to build. It makes the lazy path annoying and the honest path rewarding. That's enough for most people, most days.

Which habits work with it?+

Anything: reading, the gym, meditation, cleaning, studying, walking the dog. Photo verification and timers cover most of them; you decide per habit.

Is Broc on iPhone and Android?+

Yes. Broc is available on the App Store for iOS 16 or later and on Google Play for Android.

Does blocking work differently on iPhone?+

The outcome is the same, but the system controls differ. iPhone uses Apple's Screen Time picker and shields; Android uses Usage Access and an overlay. Apple keeps selected app identities private from Broc.

How do I cancel or delete my account?+

Cancel in your Apple or Google subscription settings. Deleting Broc does not cancel billing. Delete your Broc account from Settings, or follow the account-deletion steps.

What if blocking stops working?+

Open Broc's Lock tab and check that blocking is on and permissions are still granted. On iPhone, review Screen Time access; on Android, review Usage Access and Appear on top. If it persists, email developer@getbroc.app.
