AI photo verification and privacy, explained

Choose Photo proof when a habit leaves something you can photograph. Broc checks whether the image plausibly matches the habit.

By Shay Gavan · Published 2 July 2026 · Updated 7 November 2026

How photo proof works

1

Choose Photo for a habit. Use the same proof option on iPhone or Android.

2

Submit a photo when you finish. Frame the evidence, such as a page of notes or a cleared desk. Keep faces and personal details out of the frame.

3

Broc checks whether the image matches. A match counts towards your unlock. An unclear image may need a retake. The check assesses visible evidence and can make mistakes.

How the photo is handled

QuestionAnswer
Where is the photo sent?Broc sends the image through its server to OpenAI for verification. Broc does not save the image on its servers after the check.
How does OpenAI handle it?OpenAI says API inputs are excluded from model training by default. Its API data controls explain processing and retention.
How can I avoid sending photos?Choose a different proof method for the habit, such as Timer or Tick.

Common questions

Is photo verification included?

Yes. Photo verification is included in the Broc subscription on iPhone and Android. Your store shows the price and any trial offer before you confirm.

Does photo verification work offline?

Photo verification needs an internet connection. You can submit a photo when you are back online.

Which habits suit photo proof?

Choose habits that leave visible evidence, such as study notes or a tidied desk. For a habit without a clear photo, choose Timer or Tick.
