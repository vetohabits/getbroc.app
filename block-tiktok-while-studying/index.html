How to block TikTok until you finish studying

Keep TikTok locked until you finish a study habit. Here is how to set it up on iPhone or Android and check that the block works.

By Shay Gavan · Published 2 July 2026 · Updated 7 November 2026

Set up a study block

Choose which apps to block

On iPhone, grant Broc Screen Time access and select TikTok in Apple's picker. On Android, choose TikTok in Broc and grant Usage Access and permission to display over other apps. Add Instagram or YouTube if they distract you, and leave study apps available.

Add a study habit and choose proof

For a timed session, create a habit such as 25 minutes of past papers and choose Timer. Keep Broc open while the timer runs. For work that leaves visible evidence, choose Photo and submit a picture of the finished notes or flashcards.

Set the unlock

Use Per habit to earn minutes in your selected apps after each study session. Use All habits first to keep them blocked until you finish the day's habit list.

Test your first unlock

Open TikTok before you start. You should see an Apple Screen Time shield on iPhone or the Broc block screen on Android. Complete the habit and open TikTok again once your unlock condition is met. If the block never appears, check the required permissions.

Common questions

Can I uninstall Broc to remove a block?

Yes. Uninstalling Broc removes its blocks. Keep it installed and leave its blocking permissions enabled for your study setup to work.

Can I block Instagram and YouTube as well as TikTok?

Yes. Select each app you want Broc to block. Leave apps you need for studying available.
